So, welcome to my first blog post! Today, I'll share my Hyprland setup journey, about how I came till here and how I made a decent, good-looking setup (not for larp, but for productivity) while keeping aesthetics.

The journey begins in November 2025, where I was randomly roaming around to see some good content, and I saw a YouTuber named Bog who uploaded a video named "[Arch Linux Experience - Hyprland](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uZDPXFQYz0Q&t=167s)" and I thought it'd be interesting to watch it as it was about my interest (Linux), and it could even increase my ball knowledge. From there, I discovered Hyprland.

After that, I got a little tired of studying about it, but my small brain didn't get anything. So, I tried ChatGPT. And so I leaned into it and then worked on a basic config, which was too awful and looked AI-ish because at some point I did use AI for it.

Till this, January had passed, and I created a bad-looking config and pushed it on GitHub with a minimal Bash installer (what a confidence lol). After a month, I had already messed with my system. Nothing was properly functional, so I alternatively switched to Windows for a while due to some genuine reasons (I really hate Windows btw).

It was around this summer that I again switched to Linux (I use Arch btw) because who loves Windows, dude? Now, I was using GNOME because I like that over KDE Plasma. However, it doesn't mean I don't like KDE; it's just an opinion. We'll talk about this later.

So, back to the topic, I thought to recreate the Hyprland config with proper knowledge instead of crap. This time, I did use AI, but for the parts where I was too lazy to make, so yep. And it turns out that real knowledge actually works because depending on AI is quite childish. You should have knowledge of what you are doing.
